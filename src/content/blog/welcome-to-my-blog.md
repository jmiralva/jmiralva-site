Hello and welcome to my blog! I'm excited to have a space to share my thoughts, reflections, and learnings.

## What to expect

I plan to write about topics that I'm passionate about and that come up in my day-to-day work:

- **Product management**: Approaches to customer research, prioritization frameworks, and building great products
- **Technology**: Interesting tools and technologies I'm exploring
- **Career development**: Lessons learned throughout my product management journey
- **Random musings**: Whatever else is on my mind

## Writing in Markdown

One of the great things about this blog setup is that I can write posts in Markdown, which makes the writing process simple and focused. I can easily add:

- Lists like this one
- **Bold** and *italic* text
- [Links to interesting resources](https://jmiralva.me)
- Code snippets
- Images and videos
