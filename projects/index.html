Wanderlust

An AI travel planner that helps you find the perfect destination for your next trip and discover things to do there.

How it's built

Built using Lovable and Supabase. Integrates with Unsplash for location images and OpenAI for AI-generated content.

Plates With P's Chicago Restaurant Week Guide

A collaboration with Chicago influencer Paige Serena to identify the best deals for Chicago Restaurant Week 2026.

How it's built

Paige built a spreadsheet breaking down the restaurants, and I used Claude Opus to turn it into an app.

Job Tracking Workspace

A complete job tracking workspace for candidates applying to jobs. Candidates can track job applications, interviews, updates to their processes, from a centralized space. The project is available as pay-what-you-want on Gumroad.

How it's built

Built entirely in Notion, and uses the Save to Notion Chrome extension to save job listings to a Notion database.
