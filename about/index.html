About

It's nice to meet you, friend!

I learned two important things in college: 1) software was really cool, but I did not want to code for a living and 2) working on projects with people as excited about it as you is an incredibly enriching experience. The former I learned during an internship in Madrid; the latter writing about music in the peak blogging era.

Throughout my product management career I've brought that same ethos to the organizations and teams I've been a part of. Working in product has allowed me to work on amazing products for companies like Twitter/X, Mailchimp, Zapier, and CoverWallet in collaboration with unbelievably talented people.

Working as a team to crack hard problems and design elegant solutions that serve users and the business is an electric experience, and what keeps me excited to continue growing and learning personally and professionally.

My biggest strength as a product manager is my ability to lead customer research to uncover opportunities and quickly craft potential strategies to address them. This has allowed me to transition across industries, learn new business contexts, and shape the unknown and ambiguous into a clear path forward.

I bring my best self when I'm working with organizations that balance social responsibility with business needs and are solving meaningful problems for their users with a positive impact in the world.
